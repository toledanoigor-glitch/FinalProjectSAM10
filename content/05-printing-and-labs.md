# Printing and Computer Labs

> Invented information for a student project. Prices are in Philippine pesos (PHP).

## PrintSmart (campus printing)
- Every student gets 200 free pages per semester (black and white equivalent).
- Prices after the free pages run out:
  | Type | Price per page |
  |---|---|
  | Black and white, A4 or Letter | 2 PHP |
  | Black and white, Long (8.5 x 13) | 3 PHP |
  | Colour, A4 or Letter | 8 PHP |
  | Colour, Long | 10 PHP |
- Double-sided prints count as 2 pages.
- Top up at the Cashier's Office or through MyNBU (e-wallet). Minimum top-up: 50 PHP.
- Unused free pages do not carry over to the next semester. Paid credit does carry over.

## How to print
1. From a lab computer: just print; choose the "PrintSmart" printer.
2. From your own laptop: upload the file at https://print.northbridge.example
   (PDF, Word, images; max 50 MB).
3. Go to any PrintSmart printer, tap your NBU ID card, and choose your job.
4. Jobs are deleted if not printed within 24 hours.

## Printing problems
| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| Job not showing at the printer | Wait 2 minutes; check you uploaded to PrintSmart, not another printer |
| ID card not recognised | Use the printer screen to log in with your NBU ID number and password |
| Paper jam or no toner | Use another printer and report the printer number to the IT Desk |
| Charged for a failed print | Request a refund through ServiceHub within 7 days, with the printer number and time |

## Lab rules
- Log in with your own NBU account only. Never share your login.
- No food. Drinks only in bottles with lids.
- Files saved on the lab computer's desktop are deleted when you log out. Save to NBU Drive
  or a USB drive.
- Lab computers restart automatically at 3:00 AM every day.
- Lost and found items from the labs are kept at the IT Desk for 30 days.
- Classes have priority: during a scheduled class, only that class may use the lab.
  Lab schedules are posted at each lab door.
