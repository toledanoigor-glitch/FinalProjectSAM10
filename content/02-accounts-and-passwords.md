# NBU Accounts, Passwords and MFA

> Invented information for a student project.

## Your NBU account
- Every student and staff member has one NBU account. It is used for Wi-Fi, email,
  the MyNBU student portal, NBU Drive, lab computers and printing.
- Username format: your NBU ID number, for example 2026-12345 (students) or
  S-0456 (staff). Do not add @northbridge.example when logging in to lab computers.
- New students: activate your account at https://activate.northbridge.example using your
  NBU ID number and the activation code printed on your enrolment form. Activation codes
  expire after 14 days; after that, visit the IT Desk with your NBU ID.

## Password rules
- At least 12 characters.
- Must include at least three of: uppercase letter, lowercase letter, number, symbol.
- Cannot contain your name or ID number.
- Cannot be one of your last 5 passwords.
- Passwords expire every 180 days. You get reminder emails 14, 7 and 1 day before.
- A passphrase such as "Purple-Mango-Rain-2026" is recommended.

## Forgot your password? (self-service reset)
1. Go to https://password.northbridge.example
2. Enter your NBU ID number.
3. Choose to receive a code on your registered recovery email or mobile number.
4. Enter the code and set a new password.
5. Wait 5 minutes, then log in again on all your devices (including Wi-Fi on your phone).

If you never registered a recovery email or number, bring your NBU ID to the IT Desk.
IT staff will NEVER ask for your password by email, phone or chat.

## Account locked
- After 5 wrong password attempts, your account is locked for 15 minutes.
- It unlocks automatically. Waiting is the fastest fix.
- A common cause is an old password saved on your phone's Wi-Fi or email app, which keeps
  trying in the background. Update or forget the saved password on every device.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- MFA is required for all NBU accounts when logging in from outside campus.
- Supported methods: an authenticator app (recommended), or an SMS code.
- Set it up at https://mfa.northbridge.example
- New phone? Add the new phone BEFORE you reset or give away the old one. If you have
  already lost the old phone, visit the IT Desk with your NBU ID to reset MFA.

## Account expiry
- Students: your account stays active until 6 months after graduation. Email and NBU Drive
  files are deleted after that, so copy anything you want to keep.
- Students on leave of absence: the account stays active for up to 1 year.
- Staff: the account is disabled on the last working day.
