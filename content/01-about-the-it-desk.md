# About the Northbridge University IT Desk

> All information in this document is invented for a student project (SAM10).
> Northbridge University is a fictional university. Addresses, phone numbers, emails and
> websites ending in .example do not exist.

The IT Desk helps students and staff of Northbridge University (NBU) with accounts,
passwords, Wi-Fi, email, software, printing and computer labs.

## Where to find us
- Walk-in counter: Ground Floor, Room LB-104, Rizal Library Building
- Landmark: to the left of the library entrance, beside the photocopy area
- Wheelchair access: yes, via the library ramp

## Opening hours (walk-in and phone)
| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday to Friday | 7:30 AM – 7:00 PM |
| Saturday | 8:00 AM – 12:00 NN |
| Sunday and public holidays | Closed |

- During enrolment week and final exams week, the walk-in counter stays open until 9:00 PM
  Monday to Friday.
- The online ticket portal is open 24/7, but tickets are only answered during opening hours.

## How to contact the IT Desk (all fictional)
| Channel | Details | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Ticket portal | NBU ServiceHub — https://servicehub.northbridge.example | Most requests; you get a ticket number |
| Email | itdesk@northbridge.example | Creates a ticket automatically |
| Phone | Local 4357 (spells HELP) from campus phones, or (02) 0000-4357 | Urgent problems |
| Walk-in | Room LB-104 | Laptop and phone set-up, ID checks |

Always bring your NBU ID card when you visit in person. For security, password and account
recovery requests are only handled with a valid ID or through self-service.

## Response times (target)
| Priority | Example | First response |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent | Whole class or office cannot log in; exam lab down | 1 working hour |
| High | One person locked out before a deadline | 4 working hours |
| Normal | Software install, printer problem | 1 working day |
| Low | Questions, feature requests | 3 working days |

## What the IT Desk does not handle
- Grades, enrolment status, scholarships or fees — contact the Registrar or the Cashier.
- Personal devices that need hardware repair — we can give advice, but not open devices.
- Social media accounts or personal email accounts that are not NBU accounts.
