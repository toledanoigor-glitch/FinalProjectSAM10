# IT Policies and Frequently Asked Questions

> Invented information for a student project.

## Acceptable use (summary)
- Your NBU account is for your use only. Sharing your password is not allowed.
- Do not try to access other people's accounts, files or devices.
- Do not use NBU Wi-Fi for illegal downloading, hacking, or bothering other users.
- Breaking these rules can lead to your account being suspended and the case being sent
  to the Office of Student Affairs. The IT Desk does not decide penalties.

## Lost or stolen devices
- Change your NBU password right away at https://password.northbridge.example
- Sign out of all sessions from https://myaccount.northbridge.example → Security.
- If the device was an NBU-owned laptop (staff), report it to the IT Desk the same day.
- For theft on campus, also report it to Campus Security.

## Personal laptop help
- The IT Desk can help connect your laptop to Wi-Fi, set up email, install portal software
  and remove common malware, at the walk-in counter.
- We do not repair hardware, replace screens or recover data from broken drives. We can
  suggest what to tell a repair shop.
- Back up your files before handing over a device. The IT Desk is not responsible for data
  loss on personal devices.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can the assistant reset my password for me?**
No. Use self-service reset at https://password.northbridge.example or visit the IT Desk with
your NBU ID. Nobody from IT will ask for your password.

**How do I check the status of my ticket?**
Log in to NBU ServiceHub and open "My tickets". Reply to the ticket email to add information.

**Is there free Wi-Fi for my parents during graduation?**
Yes. On graduation day, the open network "NBU-Graduation" is available around the venue.

**Can I connect my smart TV or game console?**
Not to Wi-Fi. Register it for the wired port in your dorm room.

**My MyNBU portal shows the wrong grades or subjects. Can IT fix it?**
No. The IT Desk does not change academic records. Contact the Registrar's Office.

**How do I get a new NBU ID card?**
Lost ID cards are replaced by the Registrar's Office for 150 PHP. Your printing credit moves
to the new card automatically within 1 hour.

**Is the IT Desk open during semester break?**
Yes, Monday to Friday 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM. Saturday service is closed during breaks.

**Where can I see planned maintenance or outages?**
On the status page https://status.northbridge.example . Planned maintenance happens on
Sundays between 12:00 MN and 6:00 AM.
