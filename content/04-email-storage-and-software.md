# Email, NBU Drive and Software

> Invented information for a student project.

## NBU email
- Student address: firstname.lastname@students.northbridge.example
- Staff address: firstname.lastname@northbridge.example
- Webmail: https://mail.northbridge.example (log in with NBU ID number and password)
- Mailbox size: 50 GB for students, 100 GB for staff.
- Attachment limit: 25 MB. For bigger files, share a link from NBU Drive.
- Phone set-up: use the official mail app for your phone and choose "Work or school
  account", then sign in with your NBU email address.

## Phishing and suspicious emails
- Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected emails asking for your password,
  payment, or "account verification".
- Report it by forwarding the email to phishing@northbridge.example, then delete it.
- If you already entered your password on a suspicious page: change your password
  immediately at https://password.northbridge.example and contact the IT Desk.

## NBU Drive (cloud storage)
- 100 GB of cloud storage for every student and staff member.
- Web access: https://drive.northbridge.example; desktop sync app available on the
  Software Portal.
- Deleted files stay in the recycle bin for 30 days and can be restored by you.
- After 30 days, the IT Desk can only restore files for staff, not for students.
- Do not store other people's personal data in shared folders.

## Software Portal
Free for current students and staff from https://software.northbridge.example :
| Software | Who | Platforms |
|---|---|---|
| Office suite (documents, spreadsheets, slides) | Everyone | Windows, Mac, phones |
| Antivirus | Everyone | Windows, Mac |
| Statistics package | Students in Science, Business, Psychology | Windows, Mac |
| CAD design software | Engineering and Architecture students | Windows only |
| Programming tools (Python, an IDE, Git) | Everyone | Windows, Mac, Linux |

- Licences end when you graduate or leave NBU.
- Software that is not on the portal must be requested through ServiceHub by your
  department; the IT Desk cannot buy software for individual students.
- Lab computers already have all portal software installed.
